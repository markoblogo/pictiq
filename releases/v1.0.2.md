# Pictiq v1.0.2 — Standalone Communication Profiles and Batch B

Released 2026-09-09 after structural validation and explicit human visual acceptance.

## Architecture

- Distinguishes Embodied Communication from Standalone Communication.
- Adds the vocabulary decision tree and five communication primitive classes.
- Documents parametric color and scoped entity-symbol mechanisms without making them canonical implementations.

## Profiles and standalone vocabulary

- Added Embodied and Standalone core profiles over one canonical lexicon.
- Added the task-based testing direction and Standalone Batch B backlog.

## Standalone Batch A and Batch B

- `qual_good` and `qual_bad`: practical positive and negative evaluation modifiers, distinct from YES/NO logic.
- `person_generic`: neutral human participant for messages that must survive without the communicator.
- `state_hot`: contextual hot/heat/fire cue with the conceptual boundary retained.
- `state_cold`: cold/freezing/refrigeration cue.
- `power_energy`: electricity and electrical power, distinct from `power_plug` and human strength.

Batch A grew the canonical lexicon from 52 to 58 icons. Existing packs, layout compositions, previous icon geometry, and Handbook v1.0 remain unchanged.

Batch B adds 12 accepted reference-driven candidates: `eye_look`, `item_clothing`, `comm_speak`, `comm_sound`, `media_text`, `media_image`, `nature_sun`, `state_light`, `food_produce`, `food_bakery`, `rel_greater`, and `rel_lesser`. The canonical lexicon is now 70 icons (metadata version `0.4.0`).

## Research

The Toki Pona interoperability study helped distinguish lexical gaps from standalone communication needs. The new primitives are independently justified Pictiq concepts, not translations of Toki Pona words and not evidence of historical design influence.
