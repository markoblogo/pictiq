# Tiny Languages Protocol (Concept Note)

This note positions **Pictiq** as a “visual module” inside a broader research initiative tentatively called
**Tiny Languages Protocol**: experiments with small, constrained languages and protocols for human↔AI
communication, reasoning, and tooling.

## Why Pictiq belongs here

- Pictiq is a constrained, parseable token protocol (tile sequences).
- It has strong potential for:
  - computer vision recognition (grid/line modes)
  - fast semantic retrieval (IDs + tags + aliases + i18n)
  - multimodal prompting (icons as structured constraints)

## Cross-protocol opportunities

- “Mixed-mode” documents: natural language + embedded Pictiq phrases as anchors.
- Tooling: detect Pictiq tiles in photos and reconstruct the token sequence.
- AI assistance: suggest compounds instead of new primitives to prevent lexicon bloat.

## Boundary

Pictiq is not aiming to be a full language or replace writing systems. It is a minimal protocol component.
