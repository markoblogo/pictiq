# Debrief Questions

Ask briefly after the tasks. Do not turn this into a long questionnaire.

1. What part felt easiest?
2. What part was confusing?
3. Was it clear how to find symbols?
4. Was it clear what Frames were for?
5. Was there anything you expected to be able to do but could not?

Optional final question: How would you explain Pictiq Composer to someone else?
