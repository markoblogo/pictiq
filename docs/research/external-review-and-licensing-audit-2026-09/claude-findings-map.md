## Airport concepts: disposition

The proposed natural-language-to-symbol pairs are not accepted translations. Existing ID check: `media_text` = text/written information; `person_generic` = generic person/human participant; `move_public` = public transport; `place_airport` = airport. There is no current `move_plane` or exact document/ticket/bag/flight/gate/wait/lost/wheelchair symbol. The pairs `media_text + person_generic`, `media_text`, `media_text + move_public`, and `move_public + place_airport` remain context-limited owner hypotheses where named in the separate ledger, with no normative or human-accepted status.

For “lost person / missing person” and other safety-relevant situations, no short candidate is marked sufficient here. Until action-relevant distinctions are preserved and tested in the intended communication mode, record the result as unresolved; `LOSSY`, `GAP`, or `NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW` remain possible outcomes.
