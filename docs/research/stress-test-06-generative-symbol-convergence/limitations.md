# Limitations

- `n = 5` consumer generative products; exploratory and not statistically representative.
- The dataset is asymmetric: only ChatGPT and Gemini completed image, explanation, and reuse; Copilot was textual only; Ideogram had images without explanation; Leonardo had one weak image.
- Consumer backends may change; image generation is stochastic.
- Ideogram's four candidates are four samples from one system, not four independent systems.
- One dialogue cannot establish universal visual semantics.
- Prompt constraints influence observed convergence.
- Common icon conventions can reflect shared training data and existing signage/icon ecosystems.
- Convergence does not prove innate or universal human comprehension.
- Pictiq was withheld, but prior training exposure cannot be ruled out absolutely.
- Textual design intent and generated image realization diverge, especially for exact quantity.
