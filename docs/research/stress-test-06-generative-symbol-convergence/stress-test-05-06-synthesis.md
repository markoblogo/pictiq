# Stress Test 05 ↔ 06 synthesis

Stress Test 05 is **symbols → AI → meaning**. Stress Test 06 is **meaning + constraints → AI → symbols**. They remain separate experiments.

The shared quantity pressure is meaningful but not dispositive. In the supplied Stress Test 05 alternate-stimulus transcripts, multiple systems recognized tally-like quantities but only Gemini inferred both additive values. In Stress Test 06, ChatGPT and Gemini independently used direct repetition, while Copilot proposed unary dots. This supports a **CROSS-EXPERIMENT PRESSURE**: compact additive quantities may be less zero-training transparent than direct repetition.

It does not establish a defect. Plausible hypotheses remain:

- **H1:** additive notation is compact but less zero-training transparent;
- **H2:** direct repetition is transparent but scales poorly;
- **H3:** unary/tally notation is transparent for small values but scales poorly;
- **H4:** different strategies may suit different magnitude ranges;
- **H5:** clearer documentation/training could improve comprehension without changing notation.

A controlled next test must use one frozen stimulus, fixed size targets, and both blind and guided conditions. Do not alter Numeric Notation or pragmatic quantities before that evidence exists.
