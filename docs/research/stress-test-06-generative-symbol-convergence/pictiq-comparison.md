# Post-hoc comparison with Pictiq

This comparison follows independent analysis; it is not a design verdict.

| Concept | Current Pictiq | Generative convergence | Relation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water | `need_water`: bottle | bottle/container and liquid metaphors | **ALIGNS WITH PARTIAL CONVERGENCE** |
| Question | `punct_question`: `?` | question mark or requesting hand | **ALIGNS WITH PARTIAL CONVERGENCE** |
| Yes | `logic_yes`: check | check or thumbs-up/person gesture | **ALIGNS WITH PARTIAL CONVERGENCE** |
| Cold | `state_cold`: snowflake | snowflake across four interpretable systems | **ALIGNS WITH STRONG GENERATIVE CONVERGENCE** |
| Sale | `money_coins` contextually | coins, hand+coin, or storefront | **ALIGNS WITH PARTIAL CONVERGENCE** |
| Bottle | `need_water` bottle form | bottle across four interpretable systems | **ALIGNS WITH STRONG GENERATIVE CONVERGENCE** |
| Card | `money_card` | card or terminal across four interpretable systems | **ALIGNS WITH STRONG GENERATIVE CONVERGENCE** |
| Money | `money_coins` | coin/currency family | **ALIGNS WITH STRONG GENERATIVE CONVERGENCE** |
| Quantity 3/6 | additive pragmatic quantities | direct repetition, tallies, or unstable clusters | **DIFFERS FROM GENERATIVE CONVERGENCE** |

The quantity difference has several viable explanations: additive composition is compact; object/unit repetition is more visually direct but grows linearly; unary marks are direct for small values but also grow linearly. The evidence supports numeric-notation research, not a protocol change.

No divergence passes NEED-BEFORE-VOCABULARY. A storefront, hand, speaker, droplet, or special sale symbol would inflate vocabulary or encode a stylistic alternative already recoverable through context and composition.
