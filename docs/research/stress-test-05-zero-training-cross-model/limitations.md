# Limitations

- `n = 5` consumer AI product transcripts; exploratory, not statistically representative.
- Backend model versions and exact identities may be unknown and may change.
- Public Pictiq material exists; prior exposure cannot be ruled out.
- New-chat/no-name/no-guide procedure reduces conversational contamination but cannot prove absence of prior knowledge.
- Visual processing differs across products.
- Handbook v1.0 does not equal the current protocol in its explicit quantity-composition guidance.
- One four-turn dialogue is not a general comprehension benchmark.
- DeepSeek was attempted but excluded because no comparable usable run was supplied.
- The supplied evidence image differs from the frozen published 05A PNG/JSON. This prevents attributing the recorded results to the published canonical experiment without a provenance reconciliation.
- Transcript documents do not independently verify settings such as image upload, web-search state, visible model label, or exact prompt delivery.

The results do not establish universal human or AI comprehension, protocol superiority, or stable comparative model capability.
