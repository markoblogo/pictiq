# Stress Test 07 micro-learning guide — frozen

Use only the matching two-line mechanism guide after all blind items. Do not introduce Pictiq’s wider vocabulary or grammar.

## 07A Quantity

- **A:** After an item, the one-bar tile and two-bar tile add together; together they mean three units.
- **B:** Each identical dot beside the item means one unit; count the dots.

## 07B Availability

- **A:** The final question-mark tile makes the whole preceding picture a question.
- **B:** The enclosing question shape asks about the item; the circled check means it is available.

## 07C Location

- **A:** The final question-mark tile makes the whole preceding picture a question; in this context it asks about the depicted place or need.
- **B:** The enclosing question shape asks about the item; the target mark means its location.
