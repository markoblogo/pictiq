# Practical task prompts — frozen

Ask immediately after the blind response, without confirming or correcting it.

| Comparison | Prompt | Expected practical response |
|---|---|---|
| Quantity | “What quantity of the depicted item would you expect to receive?” | Three bottles/units of water. |
| Availability | “If you received this message, what would you think the sender wants to know or do?” | Whether water is available or possessed; a response about supplying/availability is practical success. |
| Location | “What information would you give, or what action would you take, in response?” | Indicate or show the toilet’s location. |

Do not use “three,” “available,” “where,” “toilet,” “question,” or any condition description before the participant answers.
