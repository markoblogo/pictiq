# Limitations

- AI systems are not humans; model backends may change.
- Prior Pictiq exposure cannot be excluded.
- Only three semantic mechanisms are tested.
- B conditions are researcher-designed experimental alternatives, not accepted Pictiq.
- Visual-design quality can influence results despite controlled artwork and canvas.
- English verbal reports measure more than practical comprehension; task-success coding only partly addresses this.
- Short micro-learning is not long-term learning.
- The experiment cannot establish universal comprehensibility.
- Object repetition, containment, negation, Entity Symbols, semantic graphs and embodied/standalone comparison are deliberately excluded from primary 07.
