# Pre-registered hypotheses

- **H1 — Contextual compression:** short contextual Pictiq may produce enough practical understanding with less learning burden even when exact verbal recovery is imperfect.
- **H2 — Explicit grammar:** explicit AVAILABLE or WHERE mechanisms may improve exact semantic recovery, but their conventions may not be blindly inferable.
- **H3 — Learning effect:** an initially less intuitive notation may improve after a short guide.
- **H4 — Task success differs from exact translation:** a recipient can perform the intended action without reconstructing the source sentence exactly.
- **H5 — Quantity trade-off:** literal marks may be easier blind; additive practical quantity may be more compact and scalable after its rule is known.

These are hypotheses, not success criteria or predictions of a winner.
