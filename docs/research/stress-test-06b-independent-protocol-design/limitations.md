# Limitations

- `n = 3` AI systems; exploratory and not statistically representative.
- Systems may share training data and general visual-design conventions.
- Public Pictiq material exists; prior model-training exposure cannot be ruled out.
- New chats and no initial Pictiq disclosure reduce conversational contamination but cannot prove training independence.
- Models are language/reasoning systems, not human populations.
- Self-critique is not empirical validation; revision does not prove improvement.
- Proposed visual metaphors were mostly not rendered, measured or tested with people.
- The transcripts mix independent phases with post-reveal comparison. This package excludes Phase D from convergence counts, but cannot independently audit the original chat setup.
- Model-authored Phase D claims about Pictiq can be incomplete or inaccurate; repository norms take precedence here.
- No controlled stimuli, response times, confidence measures, retention measures or small-size human tests were collected.
