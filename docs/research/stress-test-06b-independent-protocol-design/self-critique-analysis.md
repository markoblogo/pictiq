# Self-critique analysis — Phase B

The critiques are unusually convergent about failure modes.

| Pressure | ChatGPT | Claude | Gemini |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zero-training vs learning | explicitly distinguishes “easy to learn” from “easy to infer” | identifies mood, class, contact and nesting rules as learned | flags frame-shape and attachment distinctions at 24 px |
| Polysemy / overloaded operators | arrow, PRESENT, hand, money and place overload | rings, arrows, hands, spikes, level bar and slash overload | transfer/receive, slash scope and arrows overload |
| Quantity | literal marks fail beyond small counts; external boundary remains | dots, digit frames and place ticks are bulky and ambiguous | tally/bar scheme and low-resolution counting fail |
| Composition length | taxi and bottles become rebuses | common TAXI/TOILET compounds are long | compounds and nested frames collide |
| Scale | symbolic grammar competes for tile space | detailed 24–32 px failure list | frame classes and fine features blur |
| Machine reading | tree adds parsing/authoring complexity | contact, line style, occlusion and spatial graph are fragile | nested geometry and hand-drawn distortion undermine deterministic claims |
| Safety | advocates redundant prohibition | flags give/want and negation ambiguity as safety risks | splits negation and prohibition in revision |

**Finding:** self-critique supports a distinction among icon recognition, semantic-neighbourhood recognition, grammar inference, exact proposition recovery and task success. These measures must not be collapsed into a single claim of “understanding.”
