# Model profiles

## ChatGPT

- **Initial architecture / core:** frame-based semantic protocol; 22 primitives.
- **Grammar / machine:** spatial containment, modifier slots, directed transfer and semantic graph.
- **Weaknesses identified:** arrow and PRESENT overload; hidden spatial grammar; count limits; rebus-like composition; cultural assumptions.
- **v0.2 / final core:** 23 primitives; scoped QUESTION/REQUEST/WARNING/NEGATE frames, WHERE, narrowed transfer and safety redundancy.
- **Pictiq convergence:** practical meaning, controlled core, composition-first, quantities distinct from exact numerals, entity separation.
- **Difference / hypothesis:** explicit scope and relations versus flat contextual phrases; test exact recovery against task success.

## Claude / Trace

- **Initial architecture / core:** mood-led typed-cell protocol; initially “about 40,” actually 36 primitives.
- **Grammar / machine:** header, slots, spatial contact, containment and typed tree; proposed binary wire format.
- **Weaknesses identified:** repeated graphic marks; safety ambiguity; expensive number system; contact detection and 24–32 px collapse.
- **v0.2 / final core:** 32 primitives, 22 grammar rules plus 3 rendering rules.
- **Pictiq convergence:** limited practical protocol, controlled registry, question/yes/no/negation, distinct pragmatic and exact quantities, entity mechanism.
- **Difference / hypothesis:** structural explicitness and self-contained messages versus Pictiq embodiment and action-relevant ambiguity.

## Gemini

- **Initial architecture / core:** typed frame protocol; 28 primitives.
- **Grammar / machine:** LTR role sequence, attachment and compound frames; AST JSON.
- **Weaknesses identified:** frame-shape collisions, scope ambiguity, culture-bound metaphors, nesting and low-resolution degradation.
- **v0.2 / final core:** 23 primitives; reworked frame types, explicit negation/prohibition and connectors.
- **Pictiq convergence:** frozen core, composition, deterministic representation, question/affirmation/negation and context.
- **Difference / hypothesis:** strict typed syntax versus Pictiq’s flat, bounded contextual line.

These profiles are descriptive only; they do not rank models.
