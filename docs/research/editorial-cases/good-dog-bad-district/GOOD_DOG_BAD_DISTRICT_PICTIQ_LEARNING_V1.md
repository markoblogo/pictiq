## Required future production workflow

TEXT LOCK → DIALOGUE EXTRACTION → SPEAKER → SPECIES → LANGUAGE CHANNEL → OWNER PICTIQ COMPOSER → EXACT COMPOSER OUTPUT PRESERVED → COMIC GENERATION → VISUAL QA → ASSET LOCK → PUBLICATION.

Speaker species must be resolved before art generation. Dogs use only owner-approved Composer output; humans use English. Illustration generation must not translate dog dialogue independently, invent glyphs, reassign bubbles, convert human dialogue into Pictiq, or convert dog speech into English without explicit approval. Preserve approved artwork as immutable composition input and compare its actual placement to final art.

## Future Composer evidence contract — proposed data requirements, not a feature implementation

Preserve a machine-readable record before generation with:

`case_id`, `book_id`, `scene_id`, `turn_id`, `speaker`, `species`, `english_semantic_source`, `composer_sequence`, `composer_sequence_ids`, `composer_version`, `screenshot_path`, `screenshot_sha256`, `approved_by`, `approved_at`.

Additionally retain exact Message JSON where available, canonical asset/version references and final panel/bubble placement. Approval timestamp/version values must come from real evidence, not retrospective guesses. The screenshot is evidence; the machine-readable sequence is canonical production input. A future acceptance gate must compare final tile identity, order, speaker and placement against that input.

## What Book Two established

An owner-reviewed fiction production can use Pictiq as recurring dog dialogue alongside English human speech, through multiple scenes and both published edition formats. This is deployment/production evidence, not comprehension validation.

## What remained weak

No comprehensive per-turn historical Composer crosswalk, version record, approval timestamp or panel mapping was retained. Source sheets and final bubbles visibly differ in some places; none is repaired or silently declared a faithful copy here. Semantic turn-to-panel placement is therefore partial. Vetch species remains unresolved unless confirmed by owner/canon.

## Before Book Three

Resolve canon species first; archive an exact approved Composer Message/sequence and screenshot hash for every dog turn; lock speaker/bubble placement; compare final art to the locked input before publication. These are future requirements only. No Book Three work begins in this pass.
