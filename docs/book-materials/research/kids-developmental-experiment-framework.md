# Kids Developmental Experiment Framework

> Status: future research framework
> Source event: Pictiq Research Foundations — Batch 03
> Rule: no child research is run by this document; professional and ethical review required before any real study.

## Key correction

Children recognizing pictures before conventional reading does not imply that they automatically understand pictographic grammar.

Keep distinct:

- image recognition;
- symbol use;
- sequence interpretation;
- sequence production;
- narrative inference;
- dialogue;
- conventional literacy.

## Five-stage track

| Stage | Research question | What matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Single symbol | Can a child learn/use a stable symbol for a familiar concept or function? | Recognition and use, not grammar. |
| 2 — Stable two-symbol frame | Can a child learn a repeatable pattern and substitute a new familiar item? | Transfer matters more than memorized examples. |
| 3 — Constrained three-symbol composition | Can a child interpret/produce minimal event structures? | Omissions, inversion, wrong-symbol selection, compression, repair. |
| 4 — Symbol + text bridge | Can stable Pictiq symbols support concept ↔ spoken word ↔ printed word association? | Supplement literacy, do not replace instruction. |
| 5 — Narrative / dialogue | Can children track recurring Entity Symbols, event order, goals, causal sequence, Q/A, changed endings? | Narrative inference and repair. |

## Measures

Measure symbol recognition, receptive composition, guided production, spontaneous production, error repair, transfer, narrative inference, and text bridge separately. Do not collapse them into one “child understands Pictiq” score.

## Ethical / AAC boundary

Do not claim Pictiq replaces AAC, teaches reading by itself, that visual schedules prove visual-language acquisition, that logographic writing proves Pictiq is intuitive, or that children naturally understand graphic-symbol grammar. Any future work involving children with communication needs should involve appropriate professional/ethical expertise. Pictiq remains an experimental supplementary system.
