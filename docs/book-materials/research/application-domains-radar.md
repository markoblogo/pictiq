# Application domains radar

> Status: research radar, not commercial roadmap  
> Source: supplied Perplexity use-case analysis plus current Pictiq architecture/stress-test evidence.

A domain can be useful even if it is not commercially attractive. The main early question is often: what does the domain teach Pictiq?

| Domain | Why Pictiq might help | Existing precedents | Current Pictiq fit | Architectural pressure | Regulatory / ethical risk | Access to real-world testing | Potential stress test | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Humanitarian aid / crisis response | Short needs, resources, urgency, numbers, negation, and place references often matter across languages. | ICOON for refugees; UN OCHA humanitarian symbols. | Medium: needs/safety/quantity/numeric/entity architecture fits, but domain vocabulary is incomplete. | Resource distribution, triage, family reunification, status, location, hazards. | High: vulnerable users, crisis stakes, NGO protocols. | Medium-low without partner. | Refugee intake / aid distribution card with `need_*`, `qty_*`, numeric notation, `punct_exclaim`, location. | RESEARCH |
| AAC / communication support | Composition may express more messages from fewer visible tiles in situational contexts. | ARASAAC; PECS; Stroke Association communication books; commercial boards. | Medium for emergency/out-of-home support; poor for full everyday AAC without specialist work. | Pronouns, emotions, tense/aspect, preference, repair, caregiver routines, motor/cognitive load. | High: vulnerable users, clinical claims, specialist ethics. | Medium via SLP/parent review; direct user testing requires care. | Travel-safety AAC module for unfamiliar hospital/transport disruption. | STRESS-TEST CANDIDATE |
| Multilingual industrial/workplace safety | Pictiq could explain instructions around regulated signs without replacing them. | ISO 7010; GHS; workplace visual training aids. | Medium: existing safety/negation/quantity/action icons help; task vocabulary limited. | Mandatory/prohibited actions, PPE, hazard adjacency, sequence, permissions. | High if used as compliance replacement. | Medium through small training scenario mockups. | Warehouse onboarding: “wear gloves,” “do not touch,” “ask supervisor,” “exit here.” | RESEARCH |
| Maritime / logistics | Crews and logistics sites are multilingual, operational, and safety-sensitive. | Maritime English, IMO/ship signage, company safety cards. | Medium-low now; `move_watercraft`, `surface_wavy`, warnings, needs exist. | Shipboard places, emergency roles, equipment, time/order, weather/sea conditions. | Medium-high; safety-critical. | Low without maritime contact; good desk stress test. | Shipboard emergency instruction card. | WATCH |
| Migrant-facing public services | Visitors may need navigation, documents, appointments, payment, help, and language mediation. | ICOON-like refugee materials; public-service pictograms; interpreters. | Medium: travel/admin vocabulary partly fits. | Documents, appointment/time, identity facts, legal status, queue flow, privacy. | High: legal/interpreter obligations, access rights, vulnerability. | Low-medium through NGOs, not direct formal service replacement. | Town-hall intake/navigation scenario. | LONG-TERM |
| HoReCa / retail / high-foreign-visitor service | Staff and guests need fast local transactions without app install. | Menus, QR translation, pointing cards, tourist dictionaries. | High for low-stakes interaction. | Product attributes, allergies, payment, quantity, service request, “without.” | Low-medium; allergy/medical claims need care. | High: easy tabletop tests. | Restaurant/shop service card for order, allergy, payment, toilet, help. | STRESS-TEST CANDIDATE |
| International mass events | Dense temporary multilingual crowds need navigation and service access. | Pointing cards, event signage, Olympic/festival signage. | Medium-high. | Venue-specific packs, crowd flow, schedule/time, zones, emergency. | Medium: accessibility and safety obligations. | Medium if mocked; low for real event partnership. | Accreditation-card / venue-map Pictiq overlay. | RESEARCH |
| Digital / AI / machine interaction | Stable IDs and validation can make symbols machine-readable while still visible to humans. | Existing Pictiq RAG prototype; symbolic DSLs; icon classifiers. | High as research; product proof still absent. | Parsing, back-translation, confidence, gap reporting, computer vision, constrained output. | Medium: avoid safety/compression claims without tests. | High locally. | Text↔Pictiq translator benchmark and VLM recognition test. | STRESS-TEST CANDIDATE |
