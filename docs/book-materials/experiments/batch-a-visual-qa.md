# Batch A visual QA

## Question

Can the new standalone taxonomy produce accepted assets without sacrificing recognition?

## Setup

Six candidates were reviewed at canonical and small sizes, in grids, and in representative layouts. Person and fire went through multiple morphology revisions, ending in reference-driven traces.

## Result

Accepted: `qual_good`, `qual_bad`, `person_generic`, `state_hot`, `state_cold`, `power_energy`. The lexicon grew from 52 to 58 icons.

## Failures

Technically valid early forms were not treated as final when their morphology or recognition was insufficient. The archive does not reproduce rejected images.

## Interpretation

Structural validation and perceptual acceptance are separate gates.
