# Toki Pona crosswalk

## Question

What does comparison across Toki Pona, sitelen pona, sitelen emoji, and Pictiq reveal about semantic compression?

## Setup

An initial 20-word pilot was expanded to the 120-word Toki Pona core vocabulary. Mapping classes were direct, partial, composed, contextual, or none.

## Result

The full study recorded 19 PARTIAL, 2 COMPOSED, 11 CONTEXTUAL, 88 NONE, and 0 DIRECT mappings.

## Failures / negative result

Direct lexical equivalence was absent. NONE was not treated as a failed study: it separated embodied omission, standalone need, modifiers, parameters, and out-of-scope concepts.

## Interpretation

The crosswalk is a comparative interoperability stress test, not a completeness target or design ancestry claim.
