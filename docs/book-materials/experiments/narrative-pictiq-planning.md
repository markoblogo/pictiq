# Narrative Pictiq planning

**STATUS: PLANNED / NOT RUN**

## Research question

Can readers infer useful meaning from Pictiq-like tiles when characters think or communicate inside a narrative, before the reader is taught Pictiq as a named system?

## Observations to collect

- whether readers understand phrases without translation;
- how quickly recurring symbols become learned;
- whether entity symbols work for characters;
- whether an explicit legend is needed;
- whether Pictiq becomes part of storytelling rather than an interruption;
- reader confusion points;
- phrase-length limits in narrative use.
