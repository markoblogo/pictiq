# Visual Prosody

> Status: research concept / post-Composer track  
> Source harvest: [Pictiq Poetry Harvest 2026-09](../research/pictiq-poetry-harvest-2026-09.md)  
> Rule: VISUAL PROSODY is a working research term, not normative Pictiq grammar.

## Working hypothesis

Pictiq may support formal poetic structure without phonetic rhyme or spoken meter. Because Pictiq is visual, discrete, compositional, and semantically compressed, poetic form would have to emerge from visible and semantic patterning rather than sound.

This does not mean that repeated tiles are automatically rhyme. It means that artists or experiments may create conventions in which repetition, position, category, geometry, or layout perform some of the organizing work that rhyme and meter perform in spoken or alphabetic poetry.

## Possible mechanisms

- Exact tile repetition.
- Repeated tile families or categories.
- Repeated terminal tiles.
- Fixed tile count per line.
- Operator/content alternation.
- Visual symmetry.
- Palindrome structures.
- Spatial arrangement.
- Repeated geometry.
- Recurring semantic motifs.

## Boundary

Visual prosody is an artistic layer on top of Pictiq. It must not be imported into Core grammar as a required reading rule. A Pictiq phrase remains interpretable by the ordinary protocol first; poetic form is an optional convention that can add structure, emphasis, memory, or ambiguity.

## Research question

The useful question is not whether Pictiq can reproduce natural-language poetry. The useful question is what kinds of poetic form become possible when sound, inflection, and ordinary syntax are replaced by semantic compression, tile position, repetition, and layout.
