# Modifiers vs words

## Core observation

GOOD/BAD qualify another tile, while YES/NO change truth or acceptance. A semantic distinction does not automatically need a new lexical noun.

## How it was discovered

The primitive taxonomy separated evaluation, logic, quantity, and future scale mechanisms during crosswalk analysis.

## Evidence

[Protocol §1.2](../../../spec/PROTOCOL.md#12-communication-primitive-classes), [standalone backlog](../../../spec/STANDALONE_BACKLOG.md).

## Current status

`qual_good` and `qual_bad` are canonical Batch A modifiers; LARGE/SMALL remains research.

## Unresolved questions
