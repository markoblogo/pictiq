# Zero-Training Entry Principle

> Status: design objective and falsifiable research hypothesis, not proven fact.
> Source event: Research & Deployment Policy pass, 2026-09-11.

## Formulation

Pictiq is designed to support useful basic communication before the user has learned Pictiq as a named system.

A person should not need to know the name Pictiq, internal IDs, formal grammar terminology, Context Pack architecture, repository structure, or a memorized dictionary before attempting basic communication.

Learning and familiarity may improve precision, speed, expressiveness, and consistency. They should not be prerequisites for useful basic communication.

## Not zero ambiguity

Zero-training entry does not mean zero ambiguity. Pictiq may rely on recognizable imagery, composition, physical/situational context, pointing, gaze, interaction, repair, repetition, and rearrangement.

Practical communication can succeed even when sender and receiver do not produce identical natural-language paraphrases.

## Practical success criterion

For Embodied/basic communication, successful decoding need not mean exact sentence reconstruction. A stronger practical criterion is:

> Did the receiver recover enough meaning to respond or act appropriately?

This must be kept separate from experiments where exact semantic reconstruction is actually required.

## Evidence status

Zero-training entry is a design objective and a falsifiable hypothesis. It should be tested through real use, blind visual decoding, cross-model signaling, small human pilots, deployment products, and negative/null feedback. It must not be presented as proven until evidence supports the specific claim being made.
