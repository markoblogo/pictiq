# Embodied vs Standalone

## Core observation

Some meaning is supplied by the communicator’s body, objects, gaze, gesture, and situation. A durable sign, card, screen, or remote instruction must carry that meaning itself.

## How it was discovered

The Toki Pona crosswalk initially looked like a list of missing tiles. Reviewing live communication showed that eyes, clothing, size, person reference, color, direction, and emotion often already exist on the embodied channel.

## Potential book angle

The body as a communication surface in a visual protocol.

## Comparative surface note

The [comparative review](../research/from-pictographs-to-protocols.md) shows that successful systems assume different surfaces: road signs constrain a physical situation; AAC may combine partner interaction, gesture, boards, and devices; PECS is an interaction protocol rather than an icon vocabulary; and emoji depend on ordinary written language and software infrastructure. This supports examining the whole communication surface rather than icons in isolation, without claiming direct historical influence on Pictiq’s original architecture.
