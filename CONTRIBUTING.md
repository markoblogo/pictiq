# Contributing to Pictiq

Thanks for helping improve Pictiq.

## What you can contribute
- Propose new concepts (words), packs, use-cases
- Provide draft icons (raster or vector)
- Improve aliases/tags/examples in the lexicon

## Review & moderation
All contributions are reviewed by the maintainer.
Icons may be redrawn to match the Pictiq spec.

## Originality
You must submit only work you created yourself.
Do not copy existing icon sets or brand assets.

## Rights & licensing (important)
By submitting a contribution, you grant the project maintainers:
- a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, modify, and distribute your contribution
  under the project licenses, including commercial usage by the project.

Public repository license allows personal and non-commercial usage.
Commercial usage by third parties requires a separate agreement (see COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md).

If you are submitting on behalf of a brand/organization, you confirm you have the right to grant this license.

## Research archive

After every substantial Pictiq experiment or architectural discovery, update [`docs/book-materials/`](docs/book-materials/). Use judgment: archive usability studies, new communication modes, failed major visual approaches, interoperability research, narrative or merch experiments with meaningful learning, machine-interface work, protocol mechanisms, and significant external audits. Do not create an archive entry for every typo, routine dependency update, trivial layout polish, or CI maintenance without a broader lesson.
